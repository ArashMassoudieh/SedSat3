Detailed Description

Convergence diagnostics for MCMC chains: split R-hat and the effective sample size.

Both follow Vehtari, Gelman, Simpson, Carpenter and Bürkner (2021), "Rank-normalization, folding, and localization: an improved R-hat for assessing convergence of MCMC", Bayesian Analysis 16(2), without the rank normalization. Each chain is split in two halves, so a chain whose first and second halves differ (a trend) also shows as non-converged.
