Detailed Description

A seeded, independent stream of random numbers.

Every random number in SedSAT3 comes from a RandomStream. The engine is std::mt19937_64 seeded through std::seed_seq; both are fully specified by the C++ standard, and the uniform and normal values are computed here from the engine's raw output rather than with std::uniform_real_distribution and std::normal_distribution, whose algorithms differ between standard libraries. The same seed therefore gives the same numbers with GCC, Clang and MSVC, on Linux, macOS and Windows.

A stream is not thread-safe. Code that runs in parallel gives each chain or thread its own stream, seeded from a master seed and the stream's index, so results do not depend on how work is scheduled across threads.
